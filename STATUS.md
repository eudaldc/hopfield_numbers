## What the reframe did (key decisions)

- **Model**: replaced the Hopfield–Poisson (√n-width) model with the **winner** of a
  new **trial-level** comparison over the ~53,000 individual responses: a
  **super-linear-width Gaussian attractor** (Benford-frequency widths, free exponent
  β≈2) + clean-up. `WINNER_KEY = "gaussian_freebeta_attractor"` in
  `scripts/trial_level_model_comparison.py`.
- **Poisson is GONE** from the whole manuscript (it was the accumulator/√n scaling).
  Do not reintroduce √n/Poisson framing anywhere in main or SM.
- **Aim broadened**: one mechanism does **both regimes at the estimate level** —
  exact where wells resolve (ENS), approximate/Weber where they merge (ANS). The
  only unmodelled piece is the **chronometric give-up** (RT plateau above n_crit).
  Do NOT write "we only model the ENS".
- **Title** dropped "Hopfield" and "exact"; modern-Hopfield mechanism is stated in
  the body (Methods + Discussion), not the title.

## Headline results (all in `output/trial_level_model_comparison/`)

- Clean-up at **equal k** (attractor vs static twin): ΔAIC ≈ **1256** (also ΔBIC).
- Winner beats Weber/ANS by ≈4400 and the normative proxy by ≈7700 (AIC and BIC).
- **RT from the dynamics**: hybrid (convergence steps + entropy) > entropy proxy
  (≈770 summed); cofit R² ≈ 0.99 mean / 0.94 exact / 0.88 RT.
- **n_crit** (σ_n=1, adjacent-well d'=1): 4.6→10, grows with exposure, ≈ the RT-plateau onset.
- Model **matches the empirical Weber fraction** (pooled γ≈0 across estimation range).
- Parameter recovery of the winner: β R²=0.89, **n_crit R²=0.996**.
- De Carli DD (SM5): deficit is a **shorter exact code** (resolution, not noise);
  per-subject n_crit DD 8.2 vs control 9.1, p=0.004.
