## What the reframe did (key decisions)

- **Model**: replaced the Hopfield–Poisson model with the **winner** of a new
  **trial-level** comparison over the ~53,000 individual responses: a
  power-law-width Gaussian attractor, $\sigma_n=\sigma_c n^p$, plus clean-up.
  One landscape ($\sigma_c,p$) is shared across all five durations; sensory
  noise and lapse vary by duration. `WINNER_KEY = "gaussian_pow_attractor"` in
  `scripts/trial_level_model_comparison.py`.
- **Poisson is GONE** from the whole manuscript (it was the accumulator/√n scaling).
  Do not reintroduce √n/Poisson framing anywhere in main or SM.
- **Aim broadened**: one mechanism does **both regimes at the estimate level** —
  exact where wells resolve (ENS), approximate/Weber where they merge (ANS). The
  only unmodelled piece is the **chronometric give-up** (the empirical RT plateau).
  Do NOT write "we only model the ENS".
- **Title** dropped "Hopfield" and "exact"; modern-Hopfield mechanism is stated in
  the body (Methods + Discussion), not the title.

## Headline results (all in `output/trial_level_model_comparison/`)

- Clean-up improves AIC by **721.6** over the matched static readout; fixing
  the well width worsens AIC by **3488.2**.
- The winner beats Weber/ANS by ΔAIC **837.6** and the matched normative model
  by ΔAIC **11,453**.
- **RT from the dynamics**: shared convergence and hybrid links are effectively
  tied (ΔAIC 0.24); entropy alone is worse by ΔAIC 1579.5. Cofit R² is 0.95
  mean / 0.94 exact / 0.82 RT.
- **n_width** (continuous width proxy defined by σ_n=1) is **8.5** for the one
  shared landscape; the RT plateau occurs in a similar numerical region.
- Model **matches the empirical Weber fraction** (pooled γ≈0 across estimation range).
- Joint parameter recovery now fits the actual shared-landscape model: 20
  complete synthetic datasets (five durations each), **n_width R²=0.998** and
  Spearman ρ=0.995. The output has 20 independent landscape fits and 100
  duration-specific nuisance blocks.
- De Carli DD (SM5): deficit is a **shorter exact code** (resolution, not noise);
  per-subject n_width DD 8.1 vs control 8.8, p=0.018.
